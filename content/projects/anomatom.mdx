A siteswap ladder diagram viewed as a piano roll, roughly. animation of various juggling patterns with up to 5 jugglers, with polyrhythms described and mapped to various drum kits

Two hands running at different tempos are a polyrhythm by construction, and the rule that says "these balls do not collide" is the rule that says "these notes do not collide." The sound leans toward ambient, afrobeat, world percussion and trance.

Open source (MIT), a static site with no server and no account; the URL hash is the only thing it remembers. It began as a conversation about vibe-coding music, and the math is written up in the repo.
