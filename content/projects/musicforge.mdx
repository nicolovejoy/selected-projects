MusicForge is a web app that renders LilyPond charts as PDFs in any key, with many variants (male vs. female singer, clefs, octave shifts for legibility).

Setlists tied to bands allow for organizing and sharing a list of songs presented in a given way (key, clef, octave, voice variant).

The built-in metronome is highly configurable and settings persist for charts in a setlist.

It includes SongForge, a writing surface for creating new chord charts. These songs are stored in a new "BarStock" format that is akin to guitar chord charts (lyrics and chords, but no melody indicated) though it allows for definition of measures and such, and eventually may lead to a full chart writing capability with, say, a MIDI keyboard.

Source charts come largely from Eric Benson's library of lead sheets; this ecosystem will allow users to bring in their own charts, in BarStock or LilyPond formats, or perhaps even as a PDF file or other formats. Also a conversion feature from PDF to BarStock is envisioned. If you have ideas or requests, let me know!
