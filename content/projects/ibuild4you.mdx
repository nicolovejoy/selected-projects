You have an idea for an app or a website and you would like a helping hand. I built this project to help a friend build a website and it's gone many directions from there.

You take on the role of creator/architect/maker through an iterative series of conversations with an AI assistant shepherded by the team here (Nico for now). Each conversation tries to develop your idea to a point where we can build something cool. These conversations are used to evolve a structured "living brief" — a document that gets richer every session, and that is used to shape subsequent conversations and also software development, iteratively.

We've been so stunned at how easy it is to build things — sometimes the limiting factor is good ideas and the persistence to see them through. If you want to team up with us/me and see where this leads, just say the word :)

The point is to lower the barrier between an idea and a working prototype.

Danny, a piano teacher, has been building [L6](/projects/l6-music) this way since September: practice drills from his own scores, for his class. His note at the end of the quarter:

> The students have really taken to learning their songs, versus the old way where they were constantly tasked with learning new 5 finger positions and new 'songs' every week. All in all, this has been an extremely positive experience.
