A private spoken-word journal, now a native app for iPhone and Mac. It began as [Recountly](https://github.com/nicolovejoy/recountly), a web app: press record, talk, and the transcript appears as you speak. Raconte is the rebuild around one idea: the audio on disk is the ground truth, and the transcript — along with every title, tag and summary derived from it — is replaceable and can never overwrite what a person actually said.

It's built for reading decades of paper journals aloud. Entries can be backdated to the page they came from, carry two voices (the reader now and the writer then), and anchor every word to its moment in the audio. No server, no accounts — the journal lives on the device, with private iCloud sync next.

Single-user by design — it's my journal.
