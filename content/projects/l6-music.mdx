L6 is a practice app for Danny's beginner piano class. Danny built the class around learning real songs, and wanted the practice between lessons to match: slow, note by note, and visible to him. L6 turns his own MusicXML scores into drills. Notes is live, Intervals is in Danny's preview, and Shapes & Patterns is next. Students play on an 88-key on-screen keyboard (MIDI keyboards come with Shapes & Patterns), and Danny sees who has practised what.

> Attendance continues to be at the highest percentage of my tenure here. The students have really taken to learning their songs, versus the old way where they were constantly tasked with learning new 5 finger positions and new 'songs' every week.
>
> The app would force them to slow down and actually be aware of the notes they are trying to play, theoretically, while at the same time giving me feedback on who is actually doing what and how often.
>
> All in all, this has been an extremely positive experience.

— Danny, piano teacher, November 2026

Danny shapes each round in conversation with Sam, the iBuild4You assistant, and every student can send feedback from within the app. Nico and Claude build to Danny's specs, with feedback gathered through iterative conversations.
